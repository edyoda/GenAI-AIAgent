# Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform

**Location:** Bengaluru (hybrid) · **Team:** Payments Infrastructure

## About the role
You'll design and scale the services that process millions of payment transactions a day,
and own reliability, latency and correctness for a high-throughput distributed system.

## Must-have
- 5+ years of backend software engineering experience
- Strong proficiency in **Python** or **Go**
- Designing and operating **microservices** in production
- **PostgreSQL** (schema design, query optimisation, indexing)
- Event-driven systems with **Kafka** or a similar message broker
- **Kubernetes** and **AWS** (or another major cloud) in production
- Observability: metrics, logging, tracing (e.g. Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry)

## Nice-to-have
- Payments, fintech or other regulated-domain experience (PCI-DSS)
- Go in production at scale
- Terraform / infrastructure as code
- Mentoring engineers or leading technical design reviews
- Open-source contributions

## Education
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
