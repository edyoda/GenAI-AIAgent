# Resume Scoring Rubric

Score each dimension 0–10, multiply by its weight / 10, and add up for a total out of 100.

| Dimension | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Must-have skills | 40 |
| Relevant experience | 25 |
| Impact & achievements | 15 |
| Nice-to-have skills | 10 |
| Education & certifications | 10 |

## Anchors

### Must-have skills (40)
- **9–10**: every must-have is clearly shown in real work (not just listed).
- **6–8**: most must-haves are shown; one is partial.
- **3–5**: several are partial or only listed in a skills section.
- **0–2**: most must-haves are missing.

### Relevant experience (25)
- **9–10**: meets or exceeds the required years, in a similar domain, scale and seniority.
- **6–8**: meets the years, but the domain or scale differs.
- **3–5**: short of the required years, or mostly adjacent roles.
- **0–2**: little relevant experience.

### Impact & achievements (15)
- **9–10**: multiple quantified outcomes (latency, cost, revenue, users) with clear ownership.
- **6–8**: some quantified results.
- **3–5**: lists responsibilities, with few outcomes.
- **0–2**: no outcomes stated.

### Nice-to-have skills (10)
- **9–10**: most nice-to-haves are shown.
- **5–8**: some are present.
- **0–4**: few or none.

### Education & certifications (10)
- **9–10**: meets the stated education requirement, plus relevant certifications.
- **5–8**: meets the requirement, or has equivalent experience where the JD allows it.
- **0–4**: doesn't meet a stated requirement.
