# Travel & Expense Policy

## Scope
This policy covers business travel, client entertainment, and day-to-day work expenses for all
Northwind Labs employees and contractors on company business.

## Approvals
- Expenses under INR 10,000 need no pre-approval.
- Expenses between INR 10,000 and INR 1,00,000 need manager pre-approval.
- Expenses above INR 1,00,000, and all international travel, need director pre-approval.
Pre-approval is requested in the expense tool before booking.

## Flights and Trains
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6
hours or more; business class requires VP approval. Book at least 14 days in advance where
possible. Trains in AC 2-tier or equivalent are allowed for trips under 8 hours.

## Hotels
Nightly hotel caps (including taxes):
| City tier | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru) | INR 9,000 |
| Tier 2 (Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) | INR 7,000 |
| Other domestic | INR 5,000 |
| International | USD 220 (USD 300 for London, New York, Singapore) |

## Meals and Per Diem
Domestic per diem is INR 2,500 per day for meals when travelling overnight. International per
diem is USD 60 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at approved client dinners, where
it must be listed separately on the receipt.

## Local Transport
Cabs and ride-hailing are reimbursed for business trips and for travel home after 21:00 when
working late. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at INR 12 per km with a trip log.

## Submitting Claims
Submit claims within 30 days of the expense with itemised receipts. Claims older than 90 days
are not reimbursed. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly payroll. Corporate card
holders must reconcile statements by the 5th of each month.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal entertainment, fines and penalties, upgrades not covered above, spouse or family
travel, and lost-baggage items that are covered by the airline.
