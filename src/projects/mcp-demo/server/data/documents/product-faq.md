# Northwind Insights — Product FAQ

## What is Northwind Insights?
Northwind Insights is a self-serve analytics platform. It connects to databases and SaaS tools,
lets teams build dashboards without code, and answers questions in plain English using an
AI assistant grounded in the customer's own data.

## Pricing Plans
| Plan | Price | Users | Data sources | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | USD 29 per user / month | up to 10 | 3 | Email, 2 business days |
| Growth | USD 59 per user / month | up to 100 | 20 | Chat, 8 business hours |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | 24x7, dedicated CSM |

Annual billing gives a 20% discount. Non-profits and educational institutions get 50% off the
Starter and Growth plans.

## Free Trial
Every plan has a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. Trial workspaces are deleted
30 days after the trial ends unless the customer upgrades.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Enterprise customers get a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. If uptime falls below 99.9%, customers
receive a 10% service credit; below 99.0%, a 25% credit. Credits must be requested within
30 days. Growth and Starter plans have a 99.5% uptime target but no financial SLA.

## Data Security and Residency
Customer data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit. Data can be hosted in
India (Mumbai), the EU (Frankfurt) or the US (Oregon); the region is chosen at sign-up and can
be changed only by Support. Northwind Insights is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified.

## Cancellations and Refunds
Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stop at the end of the billing period. Annual
plans can be refunded pro-rata within the first 30 days; after that, they are non-refundable.
Data exports are available for 30 days after cancellation.

## Integrations
Native connectors include PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, BigQuery, Google Sheets, Salesforce and
HubSpot. A REST API and webhooks are available on Growth and Enterprise plans.
