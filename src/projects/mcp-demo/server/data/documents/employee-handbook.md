# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## Working Hours and Remote Work
Core collaboration hours are 11:00–16:00 IST, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, employees
may plan their day flexibly as long as commitments to their team are met.

Northwind Labs is remote-first. Employees may work from home up to 5 days a week. Teams meet
in person at the Bengaluru office for a planning week once per quarter; travel for this week
is reimbursed under the Travel & Expense Policy. Working from another country for more than
30 days in a calendar year requires prior approval from HR and the Legal team, because of tax
and data-residency obligations.

A one-time home-office allowance of INR 40,000 is available in the first 90 days of employment
for a desk, chair or monitor. Receipts must be submitted through the expense tool.

## Leave Policy
- **Annual leave:** 24 working days per calendar year, accrued monthly (2 days per month).
  Up to 10 unused days carry over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.
- **Sick leave:** 12 days per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer
  than 3 consecutive days.
- **Parental leave:** 26 weeks fully paid for the birthing parent and 6 weeks fully paid for
  the non-birthing parent, usable within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- **Bereavement leave:** up to 5 days for an immediate family member.
- **Public holidays:** 12 per year, published by HR each December.

Leave requests of up to 3 days need 1 week's notice; longer requests need 3 weeks' notice and
manager approval in the HR portal. Unused annual leave is not paid out, except on exit, where
up to 15 accrued days are encashed at base salary.

## Performance Reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment,
peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and a manager assessment. Ratings are on a
four-level scale: Exceeds, Strong, Developing, Below. Promotions are decided in the October
cycle only. Salary revisions take effect from 1 April.

## Learning Budget
Every employee has an annual learning budget of INR 60,000 for courses, certifications,
books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Conference travel above the budget
needs director approval.

## Code of Conduct
We expect respectful, inclusive behaviour in all channels. Harassment or discrimination of
any kind is not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with a manager, HR, or anonymously through
the ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a
violation of this code.
